## New

- **Attachments keep their context.** Pictures and videos form albums, attachment drafts stay in their chat, and pictures can be cropped or rotated before sending. By @8bury, @Rokuko-L, @eternalwaitt. [upstream #286](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/286), [upstream #340](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/340), [upstream #453](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/453), [upstream #436](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/436).
- **More ways to reply.** Reply privately from groups, forward to several people with confirmation, start a chat with an unsaved number, and open browser chat links as unsent drafts. By @OmarGonD, @eternalwaitt. [upstream #359](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/359), [upstream #336](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/336), [upstream #320](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/320), [upstream #455](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/455).
- **Voice transcripts stay local.** Whisper transcribes on your computer. Its 1.5 GB multilingual model downloads on first use and is verified before installation. Automatic transcription is off by default. By @eternalwaitt. [upstream #284](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/284).
- **A calmer chat list.** Drafts stay near the top. A setting keeps the chat list in place after you send. Mouse-side buttons and keyboard shortcuts help navigation. By @GeorgeMunas, @Nullpntr1337, @eternalwaitt. [upstream #413](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/413), [upstream #366](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/366), [upstream #401](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/401), [upstream #427](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/427), [upstream #428](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/428).
- **Useful chat details.** Group descriptions, disappearing-message timers and attachment storage sizes are visible in the client. By @LisandroNahuelH, @fgrehm, @nalyx1. [upstream #391](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/391), [upstream #424](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/424), [upstream #232](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/232).
- **Screen privacy.** Chosen content stays covered until hovered, including local voice transcripts. Emoji rendering and typed emoticons have their own settings. By @fabiomotta0311, @paulo1403. [upstream #451](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/451), [upstream #441](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/441), [upstream #442](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/442).
- **Location sharing.** Send coordinates or a supported map link. Incoming live locations clearly explain the phone-only tracking limitation. By @fabiomotta0311, @nathanbzrr. [upstream #301](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/301), [upstream #302](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/302), [upstream #303](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/303).

- **Video and audio playback.** AAC videos have sound, playback supports 1.5x and 2x, motion photos play, and audio files are distinct from recorded voice notes. By @Z1xus, @claudiocp, @eternalwaitt. By @boskowski, @claudiocp, @fabiomotta0311, @fgrehm. ## Switching from ZapFast

Quit ZapFast before opening Zap Faster. Both intentionally use the existing `zapfast` data directories, encrypted archive keyring entries and single-instance guard. Back up your data before switching and run one application at a time. Zap Faster has its own GitHub update feed and publisher signature key.

## Known limitations

- Windows installers are not Authenticode signed. macOS bundles are ad-hoc signed and are not Apple notarized in this release. The signed update manifest verifies the fork publisher independently of platform signing.
- Voice/video calling, pinned and starred messages, custom fonts and several older PRs require further account-model or platform work. The [intake ledger](https://github.com/eternalwaitt/zap-faster/blob/main/docs/upstream-intake.md) records the decisions.
- These builds and synthetic regression checks do not prove every live phone/network workflow. Report reproducible issues in this fork without posting personal conversations, keys or QR payloads.
