Zap Faster 0.30.0 adds starred messages, timed message pins, original image documents, and optional background history recovery. It also keeps pending archive changes and refused attachments through account switches, improves international phone labels, and gives each account its own notification privacy controls.

## New

- **Keep starred messages in one list.** Star or unstar from the message menu, load further pages, and open a saved message in its chat. Pending changes survive restarts; locked chats stay out of the list. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @LisandroNahuelH's [upstream #236](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/236).
- **Pin messages for 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days.** The chat banner steps through active pins, while conversation notices record who pinned a message. Expiry clears the banner and bubble mark. WhatsApp decides group permissions; uncertain requests are not blindly retried. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @LisandroNahuelH's [upstream #234](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/234); thanks @fgrehm for [upstream #187](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/187).
- **Recover older history in the background, when you choose.** Each account can enable paced, bounded requests that give your scrolling and poll recovery priority. They never move the conversation or start automatic media downloads. Your phone may return some history, or none. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @LisandroNahuelH's [upstream #194](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/194).
- **Send photos as their original documents.** Choose Document on an attachment to preserve a file's bytes and filename. Pasted images become lossless `clipboard.png` documents, preserving pixels and transparency, without promising original EXIF or filename. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @FelipeFMedeiros's [upstream #469](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/469); thanks @rajarshikhatua for [upstream #362](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/362).
- **Choose what each account's notifications reveal.** Hide sender names and message content separately, with app and chat locks still enforced. macOS also gets an unread-chat count on the Dock icon. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @BurrHM and @joseddg92 for [upstream #376](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/376) and [#368](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/368).

## Fixed

- **Archive changes survive a restart while waiting to sync.** Pending archive and unarchive choices are stored separately from phone state, with ordered retries and stale acknowledgements rejected. Complete snapshot convergence remains limited as described below. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @fabiomotta0311 for [upstream #437](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/437).
- **Refused sends recover without losing the right draft.** Attachments return to their originating account and chat with their order, document choices, first caption and quote. Recipient-device resolution refreshes once for typed failures before transmission; uncertain sends keep their existing unconfirmed state. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @terry98 for [upstream #383](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/383).
- **Presence changes wait for a successful network operation.** Failed updates retry, and completions from an old connection cannot change the new connection's state. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @Clascheurduturfu for [upstream #461](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/461).
- **International numbers keep their own country formatting.** Labels show complete numbers without changing the digits used to send messages. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @tizoras and @itszux for [upstream #449](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/449) and [#408](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/408).
- **Editing starts at the end, and long menus fit the window.** The caret respects Unicode characters; translated submenu labels determine their width within the viewport. By @eternalwaitt, with menu sizing adapted from @vncsalencar's [upstream #435](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/435); thanks @Halliday202 for [upstream #456](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/456).
- **Linux startup waits for a delayed tray, and browser children are reaped.** Hidden startup gives the tray time to appear, then opens a recovery window if unavailable. Clicked-link launchers are waited for individually. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @pedronora and @akitaonrails for [upstream #429](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/429) and [#446](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/446).
- **macOS notification sounds use the native notification service.** Built-in and custom sounds follow the same delivery path, instead of separate playback. Focus and Do Not Disturb still require attended verification. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @lucasew for [upstream #211](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/211).

## Known limitations

- Snapshot recovery cannot yet reconcile archive or star records absent from a replacement snapshot. Pending local writes and replayed records recover, but complete convergence needs an upstream whatsapp-rust fix.
- Phone delivery, bidirectional star/pin sync, actual group pin permissions, physical audio, native notifications and delayed tray startup still need attended checks. The reported [group rename crash (#313)](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/313) and [missing macOS notification banner (#346)](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/346) were not reproduced and are not claimed fixed.
- Windows automated gates and macOS synthetic library tests passed. Linux compilation passed, but tests and demo captures could not finish because of severe paging during linking on the validation machine. No Linux runtime pass is claimed.
