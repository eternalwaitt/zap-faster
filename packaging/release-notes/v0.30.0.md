Zap Faster 0.30.0 helps you find important messages, pin them in a chat, send photos without losing quality, and ask your phone for older history in the background. It also keeps failed attachments with the right draft, improves international phone numbers, and lets you choose what each account's notifications reveal.

## New

- **Keep starred messages in one list.** Star or unstar from the message menu, load further pages, and open a saved message in its chat. Pending changes survive restarts; locked chats stay out of the list. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @LisandroNahuelH's [upstream #236](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/236).
- **Pin messages for 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days.** The chat banner steps through active pins, while conversation notices record who pinned a message. Expiry clears the banner and bubble mark. WhatsApp decides group permissions; uncertain requests are not blindly retried. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @LisandroNahuelH's [upstream #234](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/234); thanks @fgrehm for [upstream #187](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/187).
- **Recover older history in the background, when you choose.** Enable it separately for each account. Requests have limits and leave room for history you ask for while scrolling. They never move the conversation or download attachments automatically. Your phone may return some history, or none. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @LisandroNahuelH's [upstream #194](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/194).
- **Send photos without losing quality.** Choose Document on an attachment to send the original file and filename. Pasted images become lossless `clipboard.png` documents, keeping their pixels and transparency. The clipboard cannot preserve the original filename or camera metadata. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @FelipeFMedeiros's [upstream #469](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/469); thanks @rajarshikhatua for [upstream #362](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/362).
- **Choose what each account's notifications reveal.** Hide sender names and message content separately, with app and chat locks still enforced. macOS also gets an unread-chat count on the Dock icon. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @BurrHM and @joseddg92 for [upstream #376](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/376) and [#368](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/368).

## Fixed

- **Archive changes survive a restart while waiting to sync.** Your archive and unarchive choices stay queued until WhatsApp accepts them, and an older response cannot undo a newer choice. Some reconnect cases still need an upstream fix, as described below. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @fabiomotta0311 for [upstream #437](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/437).
- **Failed attachments return to the right draft.** If WhatsApp refuses a send, attachments return to the account and chat they came from, with their order, document choices, first caption and quote. Known failures before sending get one attempt to refresh the recipient's devices. If delivery is uncertain, the message stays unconfirmed instead of being sent again automatically. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @terry98 for [upstream #383](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/383).
- **Online status recovers after a failed update.** Failed status updates retry, and a late response from an old connection cannot change the new connection's state. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @Clascheurduturfu for [upstream #461](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/461).
- **International numbers keep their own country formatting.** Labels show complete numbers without changing the digits used to send messages. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @tizoras and @itszux for [upstream #449](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/449) and [#408](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/408).
- **Editing starts at the end, and long menus fit the window.** The caret respects Unicode characters; translated submenu labels determine their width within the viewport. By @eternalwaitt, with menu sizing adapted from @vncsalencar's [upstream #435](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/435); thanks @Halliday202 for [upstream #456](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/456).
- **Linux startup waits for the tray, and opening links cleans up properly.** Hidden startup gives the tray time to appear, then opens a window if it remains unavailable. Opening links no longer leaves finished browser-launcher processes behind. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @pedronora and @akitaonrails for [upstream #429](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/429) and [#446](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/446).
- **macOS notification sounds use the native notification service.** Built-in and custom sounds follow the same delivery path, instead of separate playback. Focus and Do Not Disturb still require attended verification. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @lucasew for [upstream #211](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/211).

## Known limitations

- After reconnecting, a full refresh from the phone may omit an archive or star change without clearing its old local state. Saved local changes still retry, and received changes still apply. Resolving omitted changes needs an upstream whatsapp-rust fix.
- Phone delivery, bidirectional star/pin sync, actual group pin permissions, physical audio, native notifications and delayed tray startup still need attended checks. The reported [group rename crash (#313)](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/313) and [missing macOS notification banner (#346)](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/346) were not reproduced and are not claimed fixed.
