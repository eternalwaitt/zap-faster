Zap Faster 0.21.0 keeps waiting sends through restarts, downloads large attachments with progress and cancellation, and adds a chat gallery and a full-window photo and video viewer. Audio follows the system's default output, and the command line opens chats from numbers and WhatsApp web links.

## New

- **Waiting messages survive a restart.** Prepared messages wait in the account's encrypted archive through reconnects and rate limiting, send in order, and can be cancelled before transmission begins. Text, attachments, voice messages and supported interactive replies use the queue. An interrupted transmission is marked **Send unconfirmed** and is never blindly retried. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @pulgueta's [upstream #364](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/364); thanks @oovaa for [upstream #452](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/452).
- **Download attachments up to 2 GiB.** Explicit downloads stream to disk with integrity verification, progress and cancellation, with a bounded number running at once. Automatic downloads keep their 64 MiB limit. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @8bury, @dCo3lh0 and @itszux for [upstream #316](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/316), [#386](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/386) and [#411](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/411).
- **Find a chat's media, documents and links in its gallery.** Chat info opens a gallery backed by that account's local archive, including items beyond the loaded conversation. The photo and video viewer fills the window, has a thumbnail strip and previous/next controls, and keeps download, save, copy, reply and forwarding actions close by. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @LisandroNahuelH's [upstream #189](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/189) and @pulgueta's [upstream #264](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/264); thanks @itszux for [upstream #410](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/410).
- **Open a chat from a number or WhatsApp web link.** Pass an international number, a `wa.me` link, or an `api.whatsapp.com/send` link to `zap-faster`. Optional text becomes an unsent draft on the originating account, and an existing draft is kept. The existing private single-instance channel and lock protections still apply. Adapted by @eternalwaitt from @assawalhy's [upstream #426](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/pull/426), extending the fork's browser-link support.
- **New controls are translated.** The 31 new waiting-send, transfer and gallery strings are available in all ten existing language catalogs. By @eternalwaitt, reusing translations from @pulgueta and @LisandroNahuelH.

## Fixed

- **Playback uses the current default audio output.** Voice messages and videos open the system's default device and recover active playback when that device changes. By @eternalwaitt; thanks @csotog98 for [upstream #421](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/421).
- **Large downloads stay within preview limits.** Images above 64 MiB can be saved and opened in another app without entering the in-app preview or clipboard decoder. Cancellation also stops staged transfer writes. By @eternalwaitt.
- **Message info waits for a confirmed send.** Clicking the timestamp of a waiting or unconfirmed outgoing message no longer opens an empty receipt dialog. By @eternalwaitt.

## Known limitations

- Prepared waiting sends survive restarts. Attachments still being prepared remain in memory and must be selected again after quitting.
- Each gallery category contains at most 500 items. Link parsing examines the newest 4,096 messages, and an older selected photo opens a bounded page around it.
- Automated checks cover synthetic reconnects and audio recovery. Real-account reconnects, physical audio-device switching and the reported Linux audio-routing behavior still need attended verification.
- The stale archive-state report [upstream #437](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/437) is not claimed fixed. Privacy-safe diagnostics are present, but recovery convergence has not been proven.
- Windows installers are not Authenticode signed. The macOS app is ad-hoc signed, without Apple notarization. Update manifests retain the fork's existing Ed25519 publisher signature.
